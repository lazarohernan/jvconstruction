Our Expertise

Concrete Work

At J&V Construction, we specialize in high-quality concrete work for residential and light commercial projects. From site preparation to the final finish, we deliver structurally sound, code-compliant concrete built to last.

Our Concrete Services Include:

Concrete Slabs (Pouring & Finishing)

We install concrete slabs for new construction, additions, garages, sheds, and remodeling projects. Every slab is properly graded, compacted, reinforced, and poured to meet structural requirements and local codes.

Concrete Driveways

We build durable, clean-finished concrete driveways designed to handle daily vehicle traffic and Tennessee weather conditions. Proper base preparation and reinforcement ensure long-term performance and minimal cracking.

Footings & Foundations

We pour concrete footings and foundation elements with accurate layout, correct dimensions, and proper reinforcement to provide a solid structural base for framing and load-bearing systems.

Formwork & Reinforcement

All projects include professional formwork, rebar or wire mesh installation, and precise elevation control to guarantee strength, consistency, and clean edges.

Concrete Repairs & Re-Pours

We handle slab replacements, over-pours, corrections, and structural concrete repairs when existing concrete is damaged, uneven, or out of specification.
